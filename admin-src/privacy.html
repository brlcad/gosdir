PRIVATE ADMINISTRATION

Admin privacy

GOSDIR uses Google sign-in to restrict its administration portal to the account selected by the site operator. The public directory does not require an account.

Account information

The portal verifies Google-issued identity tokens and stores the permitted email address and Google's permanent account identifier on the GOSDIR server. The email address selects the initial account; after enrollment the permanent identifier also restricts access. The portal does not store Google passwords or request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or other Google account content. Identity tokens are used during sign-in and are not retained in the portal's records.

Sessions and Google

The portal uses necessary, secure session and sign-in cookies. Admin sessions expire after eight hours and are invalidated when the service restarts. Google sign-in uses Google's hosted identity library on the configured login screen; Google processes that interaction under its privacy policy.

Administrative records

Private job history records record selections, administrative actions, reasons, timestamps, progress, and job results. Public suspension reasons become part of the directory. Private explanations for hiding or deleting records remain in the private job history. Deleted directory records remain archived for recovery and audit.

Storage and control

Account configuration and private job history are stored on the existing GOSDIR host and its operator-controlled backups. They are used to administer the directory, not for advertising. The operator can revoke account access and remove private authentication configuration through server access. Job and audit records are retained until the operator removes them. Removing the site's Google connection in your Google Account prevents further use of that connection; a current portal session may remain valid until sign-out or expiry.
