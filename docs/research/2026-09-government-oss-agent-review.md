# Government open-source collections: agent review and publication audit

Research date: 26–27 September 2026.

## Outcome

This first publication pass added **25 notable software records** to GOSDIR, increasing the catalog from 32 to 57 projects. The batch covers the United States, Singapore, Canada, Taipei, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, France, Spain, and the Netherlands. It also added 27 agency identities so sponsorship is recorded at the most specific defensible level. This pass did **not** individually review the thousands of remaining collection entries. Their review state is tracked in the [candidate ledger](ledger/README.md).

Every published record passed the four checks in the [methodology](../../app/methodology/page.tsx) and [catalog contribution rules](../../lib/catalog/README.md):

1. **Government provenance:** a government or public-body source identifies creation, commissioning, funding, ownership, operation, or continuing stewardship.
2. **Public source:** a public, product-level source repository can be inspected without special access.
3. **Proper license:** the repository contains an OSI-approved grant or a clear public-domain/reuse basis. Catalog metadata alone was not treated as the grant.
4. **Primary evidence:** agency, product, release, and repository claims trace to the responsible public body or the project’s own maintained history.

The review also applied the prior [freshness and notability baseline](2026-09-government-oss-intake-baseline.md): a project needed evidence of an operational product, a released reusable system, or documented government use—not merely a repository, prototype, example, dataset, or recommendation.

## Agentic review orchestration

The collection survey contains feeds ranging from a handful of curated products to thousands of repository rows. The review separated collection-wide triage from project-level verification:

| Review lane                      | Collection families covered                                                                                                                     | Responsibility                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| U.S. government and laboratories | DOE CODE, NASA Open-Source Catalog, GSA code.json and examples, NIST, EPA, USGS, GitHub Government, and Colorado OpenCDSS                       | Resolve product boundaries, federal laboratory or agency ownership, first licensed-public year, canonical source, and current notability.                 |
| Europe                           | Swiss federal catalogue, Dutch OSS Register, UK public-sector mapping, French public-body inventory, Spain CTT, OSOR, and the EU OSS catalogue  | Trace aggregator leads to first-party evidence; normalize successor agencies and multi-agency sponsorship; reject license conflicts.                      |
| Global and cross-catalog         | govoss union, Singapore OGP, Canadian first-party sources, Taiwan, Japan Digital Agency, Brazil Software Público, GitHub topics and collections | Distinguish government-created software from government use or recommendation, then verify grants and release years.                                      |
| Independent publication QA       | Every proposed record                                                                                                                           | Re-read the exact grant and year evidence, check canonical URLs and sponsor depth, compare product identities, and challenge status and taxonomy choices. |

The agents searched selected finalists and close holds from these source families. The collection-wide counts in the earlier survey were metadata screens, not individual four-check decisions. The [candidate ledger](ledger/README.md) is the record for the larger inventory and its actual review progress.

## Publication controls

- Every record uses a file whose name matches its stable ID and references canonical agency IDs.
- `openSourceYear` is the first year with evidence of public source plus an eligible grant or clear public-domain basis—not the internal project start date.
- Editorial status is separate from measured repository freshness. The freshness snapshot records five years of default-branch activity and link checks.
- The freshness collector allowlists the European Commission’s `code.europa.eu` forge so LEOS and DSS are measured from canonical source rather than an unofficial mirror.
- Ambiguous license, product-family, source-parity, and sponsor cases remain holds instead of catalog records.
