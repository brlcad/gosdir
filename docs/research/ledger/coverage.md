The [agent playbook](agent-playbook.md) assigns disjoint Luna/max review batches from the normalized URL ledger. Completing an inventory or a batch assignment does not change a URL's `unreviewed` decision. The denominator for the complete URL-level audit is `distinct_repository_urls` in `summary.json`; `metadata_candidates` is only a priority subset, not the whole queue. Metadata-excluded rows remain unreviewed leads with their screen reason and can be assigned whenever warranted. The unenumerated collection gaps above are outside even that denominator.
