# Coordinator QA: caMicroscope

Decision: add caMicroscope as one NCI-sponsored product with separate, explicitly scoped licenses for its viewer, deployment repository, and backend.

- Government provenance: the reachable [Frederick National Laboratory 2015–2016 report](https://ncifrederick.cancer.gov/media/documents/lbr_annualreport_2015_2016.pdf) identifies caMicroscope within the NCI/Frederick research program. The [NCI CTIIP deployment guide](https://wiki.nci.nih.gov/spaces/CTIIP/pages/325550279/caMicroscope%2BDeployment%2BInstallation%2BGuide) independently documents the product-specific NCI relationship, although it currently returns 404 to the availability checker and 403 to a browser fetch. NCI is represented under NIH and HHS. Leidos Biomedical Research is a program contractor/implementer, not the government sponsor or sole author.
- Public source: the [caMicroscope viewer repository](https://github.com/camicroscope/caMicroscope) is public and documents its relationship to the [Distro deployment repository](https://github.com/camicroscope/Distro) and [Caracal backend](https://github.com/camicroscope/Caracal).
- License and year: the viewer's [BSD-3-Clause grant](https://github.com/camicroscope/caMicroscope/blob/master/LICENSE) first appears in the public history on 2019-01-25 ([grant-bearing commit](https://github.com/camicroscope/caMicroscope/commit/ec5b1525f44ef73b37d4dee50c869ce29329362a)). Distro independently grants BSD-3-Clause; Caracal independently grants GPL-3.0-only. These repository-specific grants do not extend to separate third-party services or dependencies.
- Primary use and freshness: a [peer-reviewed pathology study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609287/) documents caMicroscope use for whole-slide image annotation. The repository's v3.13.0 release (2026-08-19) and current documentation support active status. One product record is appropriate; no exact catalog duplicate was found.

- Link correction: `https://camicroscope.org/` was checked on 2026-09-28 and redirects to unrelated Indonesian gambling/spam content on receiptmate.net; it is treated as compromised/repurposed and excluded. The NCI wiki installation-guide URL also currently fails direct availability checks, so the maintained public viewer repository is used for both the canonical repository and official project URL. The accessible NCI/Frederick report remains the direct provenance link.
