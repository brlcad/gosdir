# Coordinator QA: NASA Generic Urban Air Mobility (GUAM) Simulation

Decision: admit the NASA GUAM Simulation v1.1 as one modular research product. The individual review is recorded in `nasa-luna-005-04.jsonl`.

- Government provenance and product identity: [NASA's LAR-20229-1 catalog record](https://software.nasa.gov/software/LAR-20229-1) identifies this NASA Langley MATLAB/Simulink simulation for UAM/AAM research. The [repository README](https://github.com/nasa/Generic-Urban-Air-Mobility-GUAM) identifies Langley authorship and gives direct execution examples.
- Public source and license scope: the [public repository](https://github.com/nasa/Generic-Urban-Air-Mobility-GUAM) provides source and demonstrations. Its exact [NOSA 1.3 agreement](https://github.com/nasa/Generic-Urban-Air-Mobility-GUAM/blob/main/license/LAR-20229-1_Generic%20Urban%20Air%20Mobility%20(GUAM)%20Simulation_NOSA%201.3.pdf) is OSI-approved and applies to NASA-authored source. MATLAB/Simulink and external libraries are not included and require their own licenses.
- Year: the first public grant-bearing functional source is the initial [v1.1 commit](https://github.com/nasa/Generic-Urban-Air-Mobility-GUAM/commit/794facc40155c0343a45e688945745b38691d988) dated 2024-05-21.
- Notability and current support: NASA continues catalog distribution of the exact product; the official [2026 AIAA GNC community challenge call](https://aiaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/GNC_Additional.pdf) includes NASA GUAM-specific challenge problems and NASA points of contact. The README explains simulation execution and examples.

The record covers the GUAM simulation source only, not commercial MATLAB/Simulink, other external software, or the broader community-challenge program.
